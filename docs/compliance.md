# Colombia Compliance and Responsible Collection

> **Status:** Planning draft v0.4 — not implementation authorization.
> **Updated:** 2026-10-11.

Owns legal-risk review, approved-policy prerequisites, charge/rate classification, physical-visit controls and borrower rights. This is a design checklist, not legal approval.

## Jurisdiction, privacy, and responsible collection

**Confirmed operating jurisdiction:** Colombia, explicitly selected by the user on 2026-10-11. Legal/compliance approval is still an outstanding launch gate; selecting a country is not approval of lending terms or collection practices.

For the confirmed Colombia launch, the relevant review includes personal-data protection and financial/credit data rules, plus collection-contact restrictions. Their applicability depends on activity and data purpose; they must not be treated as interchangeable regimes. [SIC: Law 1581 of 2012](https://sedeelectronica.sic.gov.co/transparencia/normativa/ley-estatutaria-1581-de-2012), [SIC: Law 1266 of 2008](https://sedeelectronica.sic.gov.co/transparencia/normativa/ley-estatutaria-1266-de-2008), [Law 2300 of 2023](https://www.funcionpublica.gov.co/eva/gestornormativo/norma.php?i=213990)

Before operating, obtain local specialist review of:

- Permitted lending activity, source of funds, disclosures/contracts, applicable interest/fee limits, and update mechanism for dated regulatory rules. No fixed legal rate is asserted here.
- Controller/processor responsibilities, lawful bases and notices, identity-document collection, borrower/third-party rights, retention, and international transfers to storage/notification providers.
- Contact channels, times, frequency, home/work visits and any permitted exceptions, guarantor/reference handling, complaint mechanisms, and evidence required for exceptions.
- Internal risk records, correction/dispute procedures, sensitive classifications, and any future credit bureau reporting.
- Cash acknowledgments, receipts, signatures, refunds, dispute handling, and consequences of delayed synchronization.

The approved contact policy must be checked before route assignment, outreach, or delivery; store attempted/completed contact evidence so parallel collectors do not circumvent limits. Tenant overrides cannot loosen legally required safeguards. Do not enable automatic third-party disclosure or assume a work address permits collection there.

## Discovered pricing requires a blocking review

The reported 30% interest over about one month is a serious usury-risk signal, not an approved legal rate. As a dated reference only, SFC's October 1–31, 2026 publication shows 28.59% **effective annual** for consumption/ordinary credit and different limits for other categories. Do not compare annual limits directly with a flat monthly percentage or assign a category from amount alone. [SFC October 2026](https://www.superfinanciera.gov.co/publicaciones/10116267/superfinanciera-certifica-el-interes-bancario-corriente/)

Calling a charge “documentation” does not necessarily exclude it from interest: Article 68 addresses amounts received without a separate consideration beyond granting credit, and specified directly connected service charges. The actual service/cost and classification need specialist review. [Law 45 of 1990, Article 68](https://cancilleria.gov.co/normograma/compilacion/docs/ley_0045_1990.htm)

Review purpose/category, actual dated net cash flows, upfront and future charges, effective-rate convention, applicable dated limits, contracts/disclosures and legal/accounting classification. Block new approval/disbursement when checks fail or reviewed rule data are missing. No override merely relabels fees or selects a more permissive category; keep payment acceptance available for existing obligations.

## Physical collection visits require eligibility, not just an address

Law 2300 Article 6 generally restricts home/work collection visits, with defined exceptions and evidence requirements; authorization alone is not a universal exemption. Articles 2–4 also govern authorized channels, contact timing/frequency and third-party contacts. Thirty installment dates cannot be translated into thirty unrestricted visits. [Law 2300, Articles 2–6](https://normativa.colpensiones.gov.co/compilacion/docs/ley_2300_2023.htm)

Product policy must distinguish scheduled debt from contact/visit eligibility, validate evidence before assignment, and coordinate borrower-level interactions across collectors and supervisors. Confirm the true legal credit category/purpose before enabling any exception; a small loan is not automatically a microcredit.
